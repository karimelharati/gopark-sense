# GoPark Sense — local hackathon app

Launch **GoPark Sense.lnk** or **Open GoPark.cmd** in `C:\Users\Rashid\Desktop\test`. It starts the saved local database and opens a separate Chrome/Edge app window. **Stop GoPark.cmd** stops the owned background processes. The app uses `http://127.0.0.1:4173`; a hosted website is not required.

Existing local bookings, balances, visits, and history are migrated in place. A pre-upgrade copy of the app and its demo state is under `outputs/before-v2/`. The existing public/private hosted version is not updated by this local-only change.

## What is implemented

- Driver-assisted gate, attendant-operated gate, and attendant without gate. Select a location under **Gate console → Setup & privacy**.
- GPS proximity, driver presence requests, attendant arrival notifications, upcoming arrivals/departures, missed check-ins, overstays, and unconfirmed movements. Desktop notifications are optional; foreground in-app alerts remain available.
- Camera/photo plate OCR with manual review. Gate requests, authorization, opening, and actual vehicle crossings are separate events. Opening a gate or moving a phone never releases capacity.
- Scheduled bookings and interval-wide capacity checks. Current physical occupancy, current reservation holds, and future availability are distinct. Shared-pool walk-ins are counted; unknown observed arrivals remain visible and provisional.
- Driver star ratings, recoverable perks, tier discounts, priority queues, rooftop access, loyalty points, ten-point rewards and free-hour coupons from the earlier version.
- Pre-start cancellation with a displayed refund preview; duration changes; no-show expiry; simulated overstay fees; verified displacement refunds and compensation; consented replacement parking and voluntary relocation.
- Audit search by plate/booking/driver, timestamp corrections, disputes, refund adjustments and penalty reversals with retained history.
- Signed offline booking receipts, device-signed rotating QR proofs, image/camera verification, local replay checks, provisional offline occupancy and an observation outbox. Synchronization is idempotent and retains observed and received times.
- A seeded discrete-event simulation with editable assumptions, comparison charts and JSON export. CLI reports are generated by `node scripts/simulate.mjs 42` into `outputs/evaluation/`.
- Paired-token hardware API, an explicit opt-in hardware-only LAN bridge, ESP32 tabletop firmware and wiring/cost guidance in `hardware/`.

**Early-leave credit is intentionally not implemented.** A normally completed on-time visit can earn its usual points; leaving early does not earn an additional unused-time credit or automatic refund.

## Default policies

Refund = paid booking amount × `min(1, minutes before start / 120)`. Cancellation is eligible only before the scheduled start and before check-in. The 120-minute full-refund notice is configurable. The previous arrival-plus-30 cancellation rule has been replaced by this newer policy.

Arrival is allowed from 15 minutes before start to 30 minutes afterward. Unused no-show holds expire after 30 minutes, without inferring that a vehicle left. Real observed vehicles remain in occupancy until an observed departure or recorded reconciliation.

On-time completed visits earn 2 loyalty points and 5 tier score. Every 10 loyalty points can be exchanged for a demo reward. Stars recover by 0.05 per on-time visit. Spending points does not reduce rating or tier.

Overstay grace is 10 minutes. Each started 15-minute block beyond grace adds 2 penalty points, removes 4 tier score and charges 150% of the normal hourly rate for that block. More than 60 minutes late adds 25 further tier-score loss. Verified disruption adds a $5 simulated fee, 4 penalty points and a stronger rating/tier deduction. All amounts and scores are configurable. Penalties settle once at confirmed departure.

Verified displaced drivers receive a full simulated refund and 5 compensation points independently of collecting the blocker’s fee. Alternatives are capacity-checked and require consent. Replacement price is 80% of the lower of the comparable normal price and original booking amount; free originals remain free. Relocation is charged at its normal displayed tier rate and does not release the original space before departure.

The less convenient Hamra location requires acceptance before booking and gives 2 bonus points after an eligible completed stay. It does not award points simply for accepting and cancelling.

Before-start duration reductions can adjust the simulated price. Reductions after check-in do not issue early-leave credit. Extensions must be requested before expiry and cannot overlap committed capacity.

## Suggested demonstration

1. Explore → reserve an AUB space starting in 15 minutes. Review the interval, cancellation terms and tier discount.
2. My parking → enable GPS, then “I’m at the entrance.” The attendant receives an alert; occupancy remains unchanged.
3. Gate console → authorize if required → simulate gate opening. Occupancy still remains unchanged. Confirm staff-observed or simulated passage to check in.
4. Save/open the offline pass. Open **Offline attendant tools** before disconnecting to cache its shell and trusted issuer information.
5. Verify the rotating code, identify the vehicle and record an actual entry/exit offline. The local estimate is explicitly provisional. Reconnect and synchronize; repeat synchronization to demonstrate no duplicate visit or reward.
6. Recovery → **Create isolated overstay scenario**. This adds a separate one-space lane and two sample drivers without resetting existing bookings. Verify the cause, refund the displaced driver, then review and accept a nearby replacement as that driver.
7. Simulation → run a fixed seed and export parameters/results. Compare admissions and rejected/rerouted requests as well as accuracy and conflicts.
8. Audit & disputes → search the booking, correct an observed timestamp or resolve an adjustment with a reason.

## Offline pass guarantees and limitations

The server signs a booking certificate using an issuer key stored only in the local database. A non-exportable device key held in IndexedDB signs a fresh 30-second possession proof. Compressed Base45 QR transport makes it readable at phone sizes. The attendant verifies both signatures against the previously cached issuer key.

A fresh rotating QR is not proof of vehicle passage and does not eliminate screenshots within the acceptance window. Replay checks are local to each verifier. A cached cancellation manifest can be stale, so offline movements remain provisional until reconciliation. A known cancellation, changed version, expired proof, wrong issuer or wrong device signature is rejected. Unknown or conflicting records go to review rather than silently charging or blaming a driver.

The offline wallet caches its generic HTML/JS/CSS shell. Private receipts, the public trust manifest, the snapshot and pending observations are stored explicitly in IndexedDB. Raw camera images are not uploaded. Device cache clearing is blocked while observations are pending.

## Hardware

See `hardware/README.md`. Default startup is loopback-only. `Open GoPark.cmd --lan` opts into a **hardware-only bridge on port 4174**; it forwards only the paired-token `/api/hardware` route to the loopback app. The driver/staff interface remains on port 4173.

Firmware uses a low-voltage LED/relay pulse and two direction-sensitive beam inputs. The device must be paired and configured before use. No real barrier or board was connected or compiled in this workspace. A two-beam tabletop model is not a production tailgating/identity system. Reuse suitable installed hardware where possible; supplier prices, installation and recurring costs need measuring separately.

## Run from source

Requires Node.js 22.13+ and npm. This computer has portable tools in the parent `.tools` folder.

```powershell
npm.cmd run install:ci
npm.cmd run build
```

For a **fresh** local database, apply migrations once in order:

```powershell
node --import ./scripts/sites-env.mjs ./node_modules/wrangler/bin/wrangler.js d1 execute DB --local --config dist/server/wrangler.json --persist-to .wrangler/state --file drizzle/0000_lovely_klaw.sql
```

## Verification and practical limits

```powershell
node --test tests/parking.test.mjs tests/workflows.test.mjs tests/passes.test.mjs
node node_modules/typescript/bin/tsc --noEmit
node scripts/simulate.mjs 42
```

Simulation results are synthetic and assumption-dependent, not measured production accuracy. The model offers the same generated demand to both policies, but can admit different requests. Rejections/rerouting and recovery effort must be considered alongside occupancy accuracy.

All payments, refunds, merchant offers and normal gate-opening controls are simulated. GPS is foreground browser assistance and needs permission; plate OCR needs its recognition model on first use. Sample-driver switching is a private single-operator demo, not production driver/staff authentication. Real deployment needs authenticated roles, a complete vehicle feed, validated local plates, reliable gate sensing, payment integration, merchant agreements and field testing.
